PhotoSize

An iPhone app that shrinks many photos at once to JPEGs at or under 200KB, 500KB, 1MB, or a size you enter (50KB–10MB). Photos are processed on your iPhone and never uploaded.

FAQ

Does it change my original photos?
No. Originals stay as they are. Results are saved to Photos as new photos, or shared or saved to Files.
A photo shows “Failed”.
This appears when a photo still doesn’t fit after lowering quality and resolution to the minimum (480 pixels on the long side), or when the file is damaged. Try a larger target size.
The transparent background of my PNG turned white.
JPEG doesn’t support transparency, so transparent areas become white.
How many photos are free?
Your first 3 photos (successful conversions) are free. After that, a one-time “Unlimited” purchase removes the limit. It’s not a subscription.
I got a new iPhone.
Sign in with the same Apple Account and tap “Restore Purchase” in the app.
Saving to Photos doesn’t work.
In Settings > PhotoSize > Photos, choose “Add Photos Only”.
Do I need an internet connection?
Not for converting. The app connects to the App Store only to purchase or restore.
Will it meet every upload requirement?
PhotoSize guarantees only the file size (KB = 1,000 bytes). Check other requirements, such as resolution, aspect ratio or background color, yourself.
