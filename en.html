PhotoSize Support

An iPhone app that shrinks many photos at once into JPEGs at or under 200KB, 500KB, 1MB, or a size you enter (50KB–10MB). Handy for application forms, ID photo uploads, and any site with a file size limit. Photos are processed only on your iPhone and are never uploaded to a server.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does PhotoSize change my original photos?
No. Your originals are never changed or deleted. Results are saved as new photos in Photos, or you can share them or save them to Files.
A photo says "Failed."
PhotoSize lowers JPEG quality first, then resolution, but never below 480 pixels on the long edge. If the photo still doesn't fit the target size, or the file is damaged, it's marked Failed. Tap the photo to see why, and try a larger target size.
The transparent background of my PNG turned white.
JPEG doesn't support transparency, so transparent areas become white.
How many photos can I convert for free?
Your first 3 photos are free, in total (not per day). Only successful conversions count, and converting the same photo again at a different size counts as another one. After that, a one-time purchase of Unlimited removes the limit. It's not a subscription.
I got a new iPhone.
Sign in with the same Apple Account, then tap Restore Purchases in PhotoSize.
Converted photos won't save to Photos.
Go to Settings > Apps > PhotoSize > Photos (on iOS 17, Settings > PhotoSize > Photos) and choose Add Photos Only.
Do I need an internet connection?
Not for converting. PhotoSize connects to the App Store only when you buy or restore a purchase.
Will my photo meet all of the site's requirements?
PhotoSize guarantees only the file size: each result is at or under the size you chose (1KB = 1,000 bytes, 1MB = 1,000,000 bytes). Please check the site's other rules yourself, such as resolution, aspect ratio, and background color.
