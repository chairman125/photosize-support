Privacy Policy

PhotoSize (iPhone, Android) · Effective November 15, 2026

Data collection

PhotoSize does not collect personal data. It has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and the developer runs no server.

Photos

Photos you select are converted on your device (iPhone or Android device) and never uploaded. Originals are not modified. Results are added to Photos or shared only when you choose to. Location (GPS) and EXIF data are removed from results.

PDFs

PDFs you select are converted on your device and never uploaded. Originals are not modified. Document info such as title and author is not copied to results. A PDF already within the target size is copied unchanged, so it keeps its original document info.

Videos

Videos you select are converted on your device and never uploaded. Originals are not modified. Results (MP4) are added to Photos, shared, or saved to Files only when you choose to. Location (GPS) and similar metadata are not written to results. Before converting, the app checks the free storage on your device to make sure the result fits; this value never leaves the device.

Permissions

iPhone: Photo library "Add Photos" access is used only to save converted photos and videos. The system photo picker gives the app access only to the items you pick.

Android: the app requests no permissions and has no internet access. It can open only the photos and videos you pick in the system photo picker and the files you choose with Import from Files. Results are saved as new files in the PhotoSize album in your gallery (photos and videos) or in Download/PhotoSize (PDFs) only when you tap Save.

Data stored on the device

The number of free conversions used (one number for photos and one for videos) is stored in the iPhone Keychain. If iCloud Keychain is on, Apple may sync it between your devices; the developer cannot receive or see it. The last custom target size you entered for photos, and the last target size you chose or entered for videos, are kept in the app’s settings on the device.

On Android, the last target size you chose is kept in the app’s settings on the device, and results are kept briefly in the app’s private temporary folder so you can share them. The app deletes these temporary files after a day, and they are removed when you uninstall the app.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by the Apple App Store on iPhone and by Google Play on Android. The developer does not receive payment information.
