Privacy Policy

PhotoSize · Effective October 12, 2026

Data collection

PhotoSize does not collect personal data. It has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and the developer runs no server.

Photos

Photos you select are converted on your iPhone and never uploaded. Originals are not modified. Results are added to Photos or shared only when you choose to. Location (GPS) and EXIF data are removed from results.

Permissions

Photo library "Add Photos" access is used only to save converted photos. The system photo picker gives the app access only to the photos you pick.

Data stored on the device

The number of free conversions used (a single number) is stored in the iPhone Keychain. If iCloud Keychain is on, Apple may sync it between your devices; the developer cannot receive or see it.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by the Apple App Store. The developer does not receive payment information.
